Fast, free, offline point of sale

KGPOS is a modern POS built with Electron + React + SQLite. Ring up sales, scan barcodes, track stock and print receipts — right on your own machine, no internet or cloud required.

Everything a register needs

Products

Add, edit and manage products with barcode, category, price, cost and low-stock alerts.

Fast checkout

Cart-based checkout with product search and USB barcode scanning. Cash, card or mobile payment with auto change calculation.

Sales & reports

Browse past transactions, filter by date, export to CSV. See today's revenue on the dashboard.

Receipts

Print a receipt for every completed sale.

Users & roles

Role-based accounts (admin, manager, cashier) with secure password hashing.

Local & private

SQLite database on the business's own machine. No server, no cloud uploading.
